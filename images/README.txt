Put your photos in this folder using these exact names
(lowercase, matching the CONFIG block in index.html):

  hero.jpg      - large faint photo behind the names in the hero
  story-1.jpg   - story timeline, milestone 1
  story-2.jpg   - story timeline, milestone 2
  story-3.jpg   - story timeline, milestone 3
  story-4.jpg   - story timeline, milestone 4
  1.jpg         - gallery carousel photo 1
  2.jpg         - gallery carousel photo 2
  3.jpg         - gallery carousel photo 3
  4.jpg         - gallery carousel photo 4

Notes:
- Filenames are case-sensitive on GitHub: Hero.JPG is NOT hero.jpg.
- Keep images ~1500px wide and compressed so the page loads fast on phones.
- To use different names or add more photos, edit the matching lines
  in the CONFIG block near the bottom of index.html.
You can delete this README.txt once your photos are in.
